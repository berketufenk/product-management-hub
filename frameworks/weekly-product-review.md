# Weekly Product Evidence Review

## Purpose

A short operating cadence for translating product signals into explicit investigation or decision work. It is designed for B2B product teams where usage patterns, customer context, and delivery constraints must be considered together.

## Inputs

- a small, stable KPI set linked to active product questions
- flagged exceptions or changes by segment
- recent discovery or support themes in anonymized form
- current experiment or decision log updates

## Review Agenda

| Time | Step | Output |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 5 min | Verify data freshness and known gaps | Confidence statement |
| 10 min | Review material metric or exception movement | Shortlist of signals |
| 10 min | Add customer and operational context | Plausible explanations |
| 10 min | Choose actions or explicit non-actions | Decision log entries |
| 5 min | Assign evidence needed next | Owners and review date |

## Decision Outcomes

A reviewed signal should conclude with one of these outcomes:

- investigate through discovery or further analysis
- prioritize a defined product or instrumentation change
- monitor with a stated threshold
- defer with rationale
- reject as outside current strategy

## AI Boundary

AI may prepare a summary of changed signals or group anonymized inputs. It should not choose the outcome or write a final decision without human review.
