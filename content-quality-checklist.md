# Content Quality Checklist

Use this checklist before a case study or supporting artifact is proposed for publication.

## Scenario Integrity

- [ ] The scenario is labeled `simulated` or `anonymized`.
- [ ] No outcome, employer experience, customer result, or commercial impact is fabricated.
- [ ] Any synthetic numbers are clearly illustrative rather than presented as measured performance.
- [ ] The product question is concrete enough to support a decision.

## Product Reasoning

- [ ] The artifact states assumptions, constraints, non-goals, and risks.
- [ ] Trade-offs are visible; the recommendation is not framed as inevitable.
- [ ] Metrics connect to an explicit decision or operating action.
- [ ] AI-supported steps are separated from human-owned judgment.

## Analytical Quality

- [ ] SQL or calculations document the assumed schema and dialect.
- [ ] Measures distinguish activity from meaningful outcome where relevant.
- [ ] Segmentation is appropriate for the B2B context.
- [ ] Limitations and data-quality risks are stated.

## Writing Quality

- [ ] The content avoids generic trend claims and unsupported company examples.
- [ ] The tone is practical and does not imitate personal experience that is not documented.
- [ ] The structure fits the artifact rather than repeating a blog template.
- [ ] Terminology and metadata match repository conventions.

## Publication Safety

- [ ] No secrets, credentials, customer identifiers, private emails, or confidential text are present.
- [ ] Links and cited sources have been checked by a human reviewer.
- [ ] The change is submitted through a branch and pull request, not directly to `main`.
- [ ] A human has approved the final public narrative.
