# Decision Log

| Date | Decision | Rationale | Trade-off | Review trigger |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2026-05-26 | Use AI for suggested theme assignment, not priority ranking. | Grouping repetitive inputs is reviewable; roadmap priority requires strategy and delivery context. | PM still performs scoring and validation work. | Reliable theme review does not justify removing accountability. |
| 2026-05-26 | Count unique affected accounts alongside feedback volume. | Multiple records from one account should not appear as broad demand. | May understate severe repeated friction in a strategic account. | Add severity context without collapsing breadth and intensity. |
| 2026-05-26 | Include `defer` and `reject` as valid outcomes. | A healthy review process should make non-investment decisions visible. | Requires explaining decisions to stakeholders. | Review if rejected themes repeatedly reappear with new evidence. |
| 2026-05-26 | Require synthetic or anonymized evidence in portfolio outputs. | Demonstrates the method without exposing customer information. | Reduces richness of example narratives. | No relaxation for public artifacts. |

## Review Questions

- Does the theme taxonomy represent customer jobs rather than requested features?
- Can reviewers distinguish segment breadth from commercial exposure?
- Are uncertainty and conflicting evidence visible enough to support a defensible decision?

## Decision Principle

Evidence should become easier to inspect, not easier to overstate.
