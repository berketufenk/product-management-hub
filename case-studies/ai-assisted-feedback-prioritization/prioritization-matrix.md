Scoring dimensions: customer pain (`1-5`), breadth of evidence (`1-5`), strategic fit (`1-5`), confidence (`1-5`), delivery effort (`1-5`, higher means harder).

## How To Use This Matrix

1. Confirm theme assignments through source sampling.
2. Review unique-account breadth and segment distribution.
3. Add behavior or support evidence where it materially changes confidence.
4. Record a decision and uncertainty, not just a numeric ranking.
